## Curriculum source

The curriculum across all 30 lessons is derived from Mike Dane's C++ course, originally published by **freeCodeCamp.org**.

> **C++ Tutorial for Beginners – Full Course**  
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLnPwxZdW4Y)

The full video transcript was used as the primary source for lesson explanations, code examples, and exercise structure. The material was organised into an interactive curriculum with exercises, quizzes, and starter code, with assistance from Gemini and Claude.

The resulting lessons adapt the source material to the platform's format rather than reproducing the original course as a video transcript.

## Capstone projects

The four capstone projects are adapted from original C++ programs written by **Luis Foster** (ofoster.2504054@stu.cu.edu.ng).

The projects are a terminal Wordle game, a 2D elevation heat map renderer, a Boolean algebra evaluator, and a Karnaugh map visualiser. They form the final stage of the curriculum, allowing learners to apply concepts from earlier lessons to more substantial programs.

## Layout reference

The navigation and content layout takes direct inspiration from **[W3Schools](https://www.w3schools.com/)**, particularly its side navigation and main content area. C++ Core adapts this familiar structure for a locally executable learning environment.
